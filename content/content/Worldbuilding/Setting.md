## Realm of Strife

> _The simulation of war_

```
Remnants of battles long forgotten conjure into reality, creating a world between life and death.

War begets Mana and Mana begets war, a world where the fallen shall rise and fight once again.
```

> The realm is manifested through past battles. The clashing of weapons and the shedding of blood propagates Mana, further sustaining the realm's very existence - this is the cycle of strife.
