## Mana

> _The wellspring of power_

```
Mana is a translucent, colorless essence found in battle-scarred regions. It manifests into fountains with diverging ley lines known as "Conduits".

Mana is the breath that gives the realm its form and the force that empowers those who wield it. Harness this power and carve your path to victory.
```
