## Warrior

> _The inventor of war_

```
Warriors throughout eons fight for eternity, waging endless wars in pursuit of glory and power - bound in eternal conflict, whether it is a curse or a blessing depends on who tells the story.
```

> The constant battle determines the strong from the weak and cowardly. Those who persevere within the eternal rapture of their own undertaking are deemed worthy of becoming **warriors**.

---

#### - Defeat

```
In this realm, death holds no dominion. Any warrior with the will to fight and whose vessel still remains shall be reborn. Only those who relinquish their hearts or are bereft of a vessel drift away.
```

> The triumphant one edges closer to glory, relishing not only pride but also Mana in the form of experience. The vanquished one returns stronger for long as the resolve burns within the heart.
